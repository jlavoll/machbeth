# 🌆 CYBERPUNK MACBETH: LORE & NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE

## 1. WORLD & FACTION OVERVIEW

### The Spire Grid & Duncan Dynamics
The city is an all-encompassing, hyper-dense megastructure grid governed by **Duncan Dynamics** — a megacorporation controlling power, infrastructure, physical transport, and neural bandwidth. City avenues act simultaneously as vehicular transit corridors and physical high-speed data channels.

### The Power Vacuum & Sector Hierarchy
* **Glamis Security District**: Base enforcer territory; industrial perimeter grid.
* **Cawdor Logistics Hub**: Central transport and freight network; crucial node for city-wide resources.
* **Fife Patrol Zone**: High-security perimeter controlled by Fife Security forces.
* **Dunsinane Spire Core**: The executive penthouse spire atop the city grid where the CEO sits.

## 3. GARAGE, VEHICLES & CYBERNETIC UPGRADES

### The Pit (Garage Hub)
Located beneath the Central Highway, **The Pit** acts as the player's primary operational base between Mack's grand battles.

### Vehicle Fleet System
* **Tactical Intercept Car (Banquo's Rig)**: Light, high-speed, agile vehicle designed for weaving through city grid traffic, intercepting corporate limos, gathering resources, and escaping security cordons.
* **Frontline War-Rig (Mack's Rig)**: Heavily armored assault machine equipped with Gatling hardpoints, EMP cannons, and Nitrous Overdrive designed specifically for Mack's Grand Battles.

### Cyborg Modular Upgrades (Mack's Stack & Implants)
* **Neural Processing Cores**: Boosts ATB gauge fill speed; increases susceptibility to `B_ANKES_GHOST.EXE` static overlays.
* **Ocular Target Scopes**: Highlights enemy structural weak points through windshield view; causes false HUD target spawns when overheated.
* **Sub-Dermal Armor & Core Heat Sinks**: Reduces incoming damage and thermal cooldowns during cockpit ATB encounters.

## 4. HARDWARE DEGRADATION (THE GLITCH GAUGE)

As Mack accepts higher-tier corporate cyberware and heavy overclocks to win Grand Battles, his neural interface suffers from severe thermal runaway:
* **Passive Decay**: Glitch level decays slowly over time, but spikes when using Overclock abilities.
* **HUD Hallucinations**: Phantoms appear on the tactical minimap and windshield combat view.
* **`B_ANKES_GHOST.EXE`**: Corrupted code payload that spawns phantom dashboard buttons and distorts weapon lock-on accuracy.

## 5. CAMPAIGN FLOW & 15-MINUTE MICRO-MISSION LOOP

### Phase A: Gathering & Tactical Phase (Banquo / The Pit)
1. **Briefing & Lore**: Receive darkweb intel logs from Lady M, cryptic leaks from The Norns, or banter with Porter at The Pit.
2. **City Grid Scouting**: Drive Banquo's intercept car through ambient traffic to gather data-cores, salvage scrap, or bypass police blockades.
3. **Tactical Intercept**: Small-scale 1-on-1 battle (intercepting a corporate limo, drone courier, or rival squad) to secure weapon parts and credits.
4. **Garage Maintenance**: Tune vehicles, upgrade Mack's cyborg parts, and prep Mack's rig.

### Phase B: Grand Campaign Battle (Mack)
1. **Deployment**: Send Mack away on a major sector takeover operation / autobattle milestone.
2. **Cockpit ATB Boss Duel**: Multi-stage First-Person Cockpit battle against rival corporate commanders or heavy war-rigs.
3. **Narrative Milestone**: Win sector, advance the story arc, trigger Norn prophecies, and observe Mack's mental state degrade.
